Meet Your Skin Specialist

Dr. Sana Waqar Qureshi — Consultant Clinical & Cosmetic Dermatologist | Aesthetic Physician

Dr. Sana Waqar Qureshi qualified MBBS from Army Medical College in 2014 and completed her FCPS in Dermatology at Fauji Foundation Hospital, Rawalpindi, in 2022. She is an AAMA certified aesthetic physician, founder and lead dermatologist at Aesthetic Evolution, and Senior Registrar at HBS Medical College. She sees both medical and cosmetic cases at the DHA Phase 1 clinic, and sets out a course of treatment after examining the skin in person.
